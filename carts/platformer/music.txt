# carts/platformer -- the theme. Rebuild with: quarp audio build carts/platformer
#
# Four patterns of 48 ticks each (8 steps at speed 6), looping forever: bass and drums
# alone, then the same ground with lead A, lead B, and lead A again. Channel 3 is left
# empty in every pattern so that jumps, landings and gems always find an idle channel
# instead of taking a voice off the song.
#
#  P  ch0 ch1 ch2 ch3   flags

   0   08  --  10  --   loop-start
   1   08  09  10  --
   2   08  11  10  --
   3   08  09  10  --   loop-end
