# Snake — effects and the two voices of the theme (docs/AUDIO-FORMAT.md §6).
# Rebuild after editing:  quarp audio build carts/snake
# CI checks it is in sync: quarp audio build carts/snake --check

sfx 0                     # apple: a short rising blip
  speed 3                 # 3 ticks a step, so the whole thing is 9 ticks — 150 ms
  00 C-6 sqr 6 -
  01 G-6 sqr 5 -
  02 C-7 sqr 4 fadeout

sfx 1                     # death: a pitched noise thud that falls away
  speed 5                 # 4 steps x 5 ticks = 20 ticks, a third of a second
  00 A-4 noi 7 drop
  01 D-4 noi 6 drop
  02 A-3 noi 5 fadeout
  03 D-3 noi 3 fadeout

# --- the theme ------------------------------------------------------------
# Two voices, both 8 steps at speed 8 = 64 ticks, so a pattern is 1.07 s and the
# four-pattern song loops every 4.3 s. A minor: the bass walks A-C-E-G, the lead
# answers over it. Kept on channels 2 and 3 by music.txt, which leaves channels
# 0 and 1 free — Sfx() takes the lowest idle channel, so a beep never has to
# interrupt the song to be heard.

sfx 2                     # bass, first half
  speed 8
  00 A-2 tri 4 -
  01 ---
  02 A-3 tri 3 -
  03 ---
  04 E-3 tri 4 -
  05 ---
  06 E-2 tri 3 -
  07 ---

sfx 3                     # bass, second half
  speed 8
  00 F-2 tri 4 -
  01 ---
  02 F-3 tri 3 -
  03 ---
  04 G-2 tri 4 -
  05 ---
  06 G-3 tri 3 -
  07 ---

sfx 4                     # lead, first half
  speed 8
  00 A-4 p25 4 -
  01 C-5 p25 3 -
  02 E-5 p25 4 -
  03 ---
  04 D-5 p25 3 -
  05 B-4 p25 3 -
  06 A-4 p25 4 fadeout
  07 ---

sfx 5                     # lead, second half
  speed 8
  00 C-5 p25 4 -
  01 ---
  02 F-5 p25 3 -
  03 E-5 p25 3 -
  04 D-5 p25 4 -
  05 ---
  06 B-4 p25 3 fadeout
  07 ---
